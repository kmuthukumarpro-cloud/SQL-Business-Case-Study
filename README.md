# SQL Business Case Study

## Overview

This project contains solutions to 15 business-focused SQL questions using a sales data warehouse model.

The goal of this project was to practice SQL skills commonly used in Data Analyst roles, including:

- Aggregations
- Joins
- Subqueries
- CTEs
- Window Functions
- Ranking
- Revenue Analysis
- Customer Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis

## Dataset

### fact_sales
Stores transaction-level sales information.

### dim_customers
Stores customer details.

### dim_products
Stores product information.

---

# Project Questions

## Easy SQL

### 1. Total Revenue
Calculate total revenue generated by the company.

### 2. Total Customers
Find the number of unique customers.

### 3. Total Orders
Find the number of unique orders.

### 4. Top Product
Identify the product generating the highest revenue.

### 5. Top Customer
Identify the customer generating the highest revenue.

---

## Intermediate SQL

### 6. Revenue by Country
Calculate total revenue generated in each country.

### 7. Revenue by Month
Analyze monthly revenue trends.

### 8. Average Order Value
Calculate average revenue generated per order.

### 9. Customers Above Average Spend
Identify customers spending more than the average customer.

### 10. Products Below Average Sales
Identify products generating less revenue than the average product.

---

## Advanced SQL

### 11. Rank Customers
Rank customers by revenue using window functions.

### 12. Top 3 Products
Identify the top 3 products by revenue.

### 13. Running Total Revenue
Calculate cumulative revenue over time.

### 14. Monthly Growth Analysis
Compare monthly revenue with the previous month.

### 15. Revenue Contribution Percentage
Calculate each product's contribution to total company revenue.
